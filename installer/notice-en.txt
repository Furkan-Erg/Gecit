WHAT YOU SHOULD KNOW ABOUT GEÇİT

Geçit brings these tools together: unblocking, smart/encrypted DNS, a private browser, game mode and internet measurements.

UNBLOCKING
- It uses the open-source GoodbyeDPI (Apache 2.0) and WinDivert (LGPLv3). WinDivert is a network driver. Some antivirus programs (Kaspersky in particular) may wrongly flag it as dangerous.
- Geçit is NOT a VPN. It doesn't hide your identity or your IP address; it only gets around your ISP's DPI and DNS blocks.
- You are solely responsible for any legal consequences of using it.

ADMIN PERMISSION
- Unblocking, encrypted DNS and per-app traffic monitoring need Windows services and drivers, so these ask for admin permission once.
- When you remove Geçit with "Settings > Remove Geçit from the system" or from Control Panel, the services are deleted and your DNS settings go back to how they were before Geçit.

This software isn't code-signed, so Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" warning. To continue, click "More info" > "Run anyway".
